Quantum Logic 2025-26

MEF - MSc in Physics Engineering

Dep. Informática, Universidade do Minho

Objectives and learning outcomes

The development of quantum information and computation entails the need for formal tools to model, verify and reason rigorously about quantum programs. This course explores

  • a brief introduction to category theory as a suitable mathematical framework to reason about the semantics of programs;
  • a language of string-diagrams, with a precise categorical semantics, to express basic principles of quantum processes;
  • a particular set of string-diagram equations, called the ZX-calculus, to reason and transform quantum circuits at a higher level of abstraction;
  • a Python library, called PyZX, to play with ZX-calculus in practice.
Thus, in the end, students will be able to apply to the development of quantum programs a solid and operative knowledge in program semantics and calculi.

Syllabus

  • Module 1: A brief introduction to category theory.
    • What is a category and why we care: definitions, examples, basic constructions.
    • Functors and natural transformations.
    • Basic constructions in a category: duality and universality.
    • Monads and adjunctions.
    • Bringing logic into the picture.
  • Module 2: Categories for quantum processes
    • Monoidal categories and string diagrams.
    • Computational interpretation of quantum mechanics. Associated categorical structures: monoidal (composition), compact closed (entanglement), adjunctions (internal product), biproduts (non deterministic branching).
    • Linear processes and quantum processes.
  • Module 3: A hands-on introduction to the ZX-calculus
    • The ZX-calculus.
    • Using PyZX.
    • Examples and case studies.
  • Extra: Other formal approches to verification of quantum programs
